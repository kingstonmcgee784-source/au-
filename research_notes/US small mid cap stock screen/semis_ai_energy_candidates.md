# U.S. Small/Mid-Cap Screen: Semiconductors, Semi Equipment, AI/Data-Center Hardware, Energy Infrastructure & Nuclear (as of 2026-11-16)

**Data provenance legend.** "SA" = StockAnalysis.com statistics pages, fetched 2026-11-16 around 12:00–12:12 PM EST while the market was open. These are **intraday/delayed** quotes, so prices will differ at the close. Ratios such as P/E, EV/EBITDA and margins are SA's trailing-12-month calculations. Analyst targets and forward P/E are **analyst estimates** aggregated by SA. Items marked **[calc]** are my own arithmetic from the cited inputs. "Not found" means I did not verify the item; nothing was estimated silently.

**Screen scope.** I looked at **26 names**: 6 checked in depth (statistics page plus filings/transcripts), 20 surfaced and screened on headline results only. I used about 16 tool calls, so coverage is necessarily partial. Many requested fields (13F changes, Form 4 detail, options/IV, 20/100-day MAs, MACD, relative performance vs SPX/ETFs, ATR, 52-week high/low) were **not found** in this pass and are listed as gaps.

**Most important market-context finding.** Every in-depth candidate was trading **below both its 50-day and 200-day moving averages** on 2026-11-16, with RSI between 32 and 48. Most were down 3–8% intraday. For example, at around noon COHU was -7.8%, AAOI -7.0%, ICHR -5.0%, AMSC -3.3% and PLAB -3.0% (SA pages below). This looks like a broad correction in small/mid-cap semis and AI hardware, not a stock-specific event. **None of the candidates currently meets the "constructive chart" criterion.** The ranked list below therefore puts fundamentals and valuation first. Any 1–3 month entry should wait for basing or a reclaim of the 50-day MA, not anticipate one.
