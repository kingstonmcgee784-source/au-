Suggested ranking for a 1–3 month hold (secondary 6–12 months):
1. **MRCY (Mercury Systems).** It has the best combination of a beat, a raised outlook, a record backlog and a neutral chart sitting on its 50-day MA.
2. **ATRO (Astronics).** Fundamentals are strong and the PEG is 0.95, but the stock fell after its guidance. It needs to reclaim its 50-day MA.
3. **DCO (Ducommun).** This is an oversold mean-reversion or "beat but sold" setup: RSI 32 and about 18% below its 50-day MA. The setup is speculative.
4. **VSEC (VSE Corp).** It is growing fastest and its chart is above the 50-day MA, but dilution is heavy and leverage is high, which fails the dilution screen. It is a conditional, watchlist-only name.
- No robotics or industrial-automation small cap passed the screen within this research budget (see rejects).
