## Screening approach, names looked at, and rejections

### Takeaway
I looked at 22 names. Four survive: **LivaNova (LIVN)**, **Healthcare Services Group (HCSG)**, **Merit Medical (MMSI)** and **Carlsmed (CARL)**. All four reported Q3 2026 results in Oct–Nov 2026. Three raised or narrowed-up 2026 guidance, and the fourth (HCSG) raised its 2027 growth outlook.

Ranking for a 1–3 month hold:
1. LIVN: best mix of beat-and-raise, trend and liquidity.
2. HCSG: cheap, low beta, buyback, raised 2027 outlook.
3. MMSI: quality compounder, but neutral chart.
4. CARL: high growth, but small float and illiquid; a speculative position size.

I found no qualifying name in consumer, materials or business services within the budget.
